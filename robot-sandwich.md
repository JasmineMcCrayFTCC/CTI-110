
# Ice cream sandwhich
## Assumptions
1. All ingredients are pre-messured and in seperate bowls or cups as listed:
- 2 and 1/4 cups of all puropse flour
- 1 teaspoon baking soda
- 1/2 teaspoon salt 
- 2 1/4 cup flour
- 1 1/2 teaspoons cornstarch
- 3/4 cup unsalted butter 
- 3/4 cup sugar
- 1/2 cup granulated sugar
- 1 large egg
- 1 yolk
- 2 teaspoons vanilla extract
- 1 and 1/4 cup chocolate chips
2. A freezer is available behind the large table with 1.5 quarts of vanilla ice cream.
3. A cupboard between the freezer and the oven contains a baking sheet and plastic wrap.
4. Utensils (spoons, scoops, mixers) are on the bottom left corner of the table.
5. Two empty large bowls and one empty small bowl are on the bottom right corner of the table.
6. Necessary utentials such as spoons, scoops, and mixers will be layed out on the bottom left corner of the table
7. There will be 2 empty bowls, 2 large, 1 small on the bottom right corner of the table.
8. Next to the bowls are plastic wraps.
## Actions
1. Pick up large bowl from the bottom right corner of the table.
2. Pick up whisk from the utensils area.
3. Add flower to the large bowl.
4. Add the cornstarch to the same bowl.
5. Add the baking soda to the same bowl.
6. Add the salt to the same bowl.
7. Whisk the dry ingredients together until evenly combined.
4. Set the bowl aside.
5. Pick up the second large bowl from the table.
6. Pick up a clean whisk or spoon
7. Add the softened butter to the second large bowl.
8. Add the brown sugar to the butter.
9. Add the granulated sugar to the butter.
10. Beat the butter and the sugars togehter until the mixture is smooth and creamy.
11. Set bowl aside.
12. Pick up a small bowl from the table.
13. Pick up a clean spoon.
14. Crack the large egg into the small bowl.
15. Seperate the yolk from the second egg and it to the small bowl.
16. Stir the egg, yolk, and vanilla together until combined.
17. Pour the egg mixture into the butter-sugar mixture.
18. Stir until fully combined.
19. Add the dry flour mixture to the wet ingredients.
20. Stir until the dough is well combined.
21. Pick up another clean spoon.
22. Fold chocolate chips into the dough until evenly distributed.
23. Pick up plastic wrap.
24. Cover the dough bowl with the plastic wrap.
25. Set the bowl aside.
26. Preheat the oven to 350 degrees F.
27. Open the cupboard and take out the baking sheet pan.
28. Grab the bowl that contains the dough inside.
29. Unwrap the bowl.
30. Roll the chilled dough into balls, using about 1.5 tablespoons of dough per ball.
31. Place the dough balls on the baking sheet, spacing them 2 inches apart.
32. Place baking sheet in the oven
33. Bake for 10 to 12 minutes.
34. Remove the baking sheet from the oven.
35. Place it on the stovetop.
36. Let it cool for 5 minutes.
37. Grab ice cream out of freezer. 
38. Grab ice cream scoop.
39. Let cookies cool on the baking sheet for 5 minutes.
40. Transfer cookies to a wire rack.
41. Pick up one cooled cookie.
42. flip it upside down.
43. Scoop some ice cream onto the upside-down cookie.
44. Place a second cookie on top, right-side up. 
45. Press down gently.
46. Repeat for all cookies .
47. Grab plastic wrap.
48. Wrap all the cookies.
49. Place all sandwhiches in freezer.
## Done when
- All cookies are in the freezer
